## 2. Required Legal Pages List & Disclaimer Wording Patterns

To establish legal protection and maintain compliance across payment gateways (Paddle, Stripe, Razorpay) and regulatory bodies (SEBI, RBI, FTC, GDPR), FORTREX must deploy five core legal documents on its web platform:

### 2.1 Terms of Service (ToS)
* **Scope**: Governs platform membership, account creation, tournament entry rules, REX points utility, and acceptable use policies.
* **Key Clause Pattern — Non-Broker & Non-Advisory Status**:
  > *"FORTREX ('Platform') operates strictly as a technology provider, software platform, and educational evaluation environment. FORTREX is not a registered broker-dealer, financial services provider, investment advisor, portfolio manager, or commodity trading advisor under SEBI, SEC, FCA, or any other financial regulatory authority. All services, leaderboard software, and tournament evaluations are intended solely for educational, analytical, and informational purposes."*
* **Key Clause Pattern — Eligibility & Geo-Restrictions**:
  > *"Users must be at least 18 years of age or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction. Services are void where prohibited by law. Residents of restricted jurisdictions—including but not limited to Indian residents (with respect to offshore forex activity), the United States, North Korea, Iran, Syria, and OFAC-sanctioned regions—are prohibited from purchasing entry tickets or accessing broker referral links."*

### 2.2 Risk Disclosure & Simulated Trading Disclaimer
* **Scope**: Required for all trading tournament platforms, prop firm models, and broker referral portals. Must appear prominently in the website footer and tournament checkout flow.
* **Key Clause Pattern — Prop Firm / Simulated Trading Standard (FTMO Pattern)**:
  > *"Trading foreign exchange, CFDs, equities, and financial derivatives carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. The high degree of leverage can work against you as well as for you. Before deciding to participate in any tournament or evaluation, you should carefully consider your objectives, financial situation, and risk tolerance.*  
  > *All tournament competitions hosted by FORTREX utilize simulated trading accounts with virtual capital or demo accounts provided by independent third-party brokers. Hypothetical or simulated performance results have inherent limitations and do not represent actual trading in live markets. No representation is being made that any account will or is likely to achieve profits or losses similar to those shown."* **`[NEEDS-LAWYER]`**

### 2.3 Broker IB & Affiliate Disclosure
* **Scope**: Satisfies ASCI (India) and FTC (US) rules governing financial promotions and material connection disclosures.
* **Key Clause Pattern — IB Revenue & Transparency**:
  > *"FORTREX maintains commercial relationships with third-party brokers, including Introducing Broker (IB) and affiliate agreements. FORTREX may receive monetary compensation, commissions, or rebates from partner brokers (such as XM) when users register accounts or execute trades through referral links provided on our platform. This financial relationship does not influence tournament rankings or ROI evaluation scoring. Users are under no obligation to utilize partner brokers and do so entirely at their own risk and discretion."*

### 2.4 Privacy Policy (DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, CCPA Compliant)
* **Scope**: Regulates collection, storage, processing, and transfer of user personal data, trading account IDs, and analytics.
* **Key Clause Pattern — Third-Party Broker Data Consent**:
  > *"By participating in tournaments, you grant explicit consent for FORTREX to collect and verify your broker account ID, read-only API trading metrics, and tournament performance data. FORTREX does not store broker account passwords or trading credentials. Personal data will not be sold to third parties. Data transfers outside your home country are conducted under standard contractual clauses ensuring equivalent data protection standards under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India) and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)."* **`[NEEDS-LAWYER]`**

### 2.5 Refund & Ticket Cancellation Policy
* **Scope**: Prevents chargeback abuse on SaaS tickets, REX points purchases, and tournament fees.
* **Key Clause Pattern — Digital Content Delivery**:
  > *"All purchases of tournament entry tickets, digital utility subscriptions, and REX points are final, non-refundable, and non-transferable once the tournament session has commenced or digital tokens have been credited to the user account. Refund requests submitted prior to the start of a competition will be subject to a 10% administrative processing fee."*

### Phase 1: Entity Architecture & Corporate Setup (Immediate — Priority 1)
1. **India Onshore Entity**: Form/utilize **Beyond Pixells Pvt Ltd** (or Fortrex Tech Solutions Pvt Ltd) in India.
   * **Role**: Serves as the software R&D center, design agency, and operational support unit.
   * **Corporate Taxes**: Standard corporate income tax in India (22% + surcharge/cess under Section 115BAA).
2. **Global Offshore Entity**: Incorporate a holding/operating entity outside India:
   * **Recommended Jurisdictions**:
     * **Option A: US Delaware LLC / C-Corp** (via Stripe Atlas or Firstbase): Best for global SaaS credibility, native Stripe/Paddle integration, and investor readiness.
     * **Option B: UAE Freezone (IFZA / Meydan / RAKEZ)**: 0% corporate tax (up to threshold), zero currency controls, ideal for foreign broker IB contracts (XM) and global operations.
     * **Option C: Singapore Pte Ltd**: High reputation in APAC, clean cross-border payment setup.
   * **Role**: Owns the FORTREX brand/IP, contracts directly with XM for global IB commissions, processes global customer payments via Paddle/LemonSqueezy, and collects tournament entry fees from non-Indian users. **`[NEEDS-LAWYER]`**
3. **Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) / OI Compliance**: Ensure Indian founder complies with RBI Overseas Investment Rules 2022 when taking equity in the offshore entity.

### Phase 2: Inter-Company Contracts & Tax/GST Basics (Priority 2)
1. **Master Services Agreement (MSA) & Statement of Work (SOW)**:
   * Execute an arm's-length contract between **FORTREX Global Entity** and **Beyond Pixells Pvt Ltd (India)**.
   * Beyond Pixells bills FORTREX Global for software development, maintenance, and design services on a Cost + Margin basis (e.g., Cost + 15%).
2. **IP Assignment Agreement**:
   * Explicitly assign all source code, trademarks, UI/UX designs, and algorithms created by Beyond Pixells developers to FORTREX Global Entity.
3. **GST Registration & Zero-Rated Export of Services**:
   * Obtain GST registration for Beyond Pixells Pvt Ltd in India (SAC 998314 for IT software, SAC 998311 for IT consultancy).
   * File **Letter of Undertaking (LUT)** annually under Section 16 of the IGST Act to export services to FORTREX Global at **0% IGST**.
   * Maintain Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates / Advice (**e-FIRC / FIRA**) issued by authorized dealer (AD Category-1) bank to prove export compliance.

### Phase 3: Regulatory & Operational Compliance (Priority 3)
1. **Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023)**:
   * Appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO) / point of contact in India.
   * Implement explicit opt-in consent banners and data minimization protocols.
2. **Bank Accounts & FX Accounts**:
   * India: Corporate bank account (HDFC / ICICI / Axis) with AD-1 code enabled for export tracking (EDPMS).
   * Global: Mercury Bank / Relay / Wise Business / Emirates NBD for the offshore entity.

## 4. Broker IB Relationship & XM Compliance Constraints

Partnering with **XM** or similar global MetaTrader 4 / MetaTrader 5 brokers under an **Introducing Broker (IB)** or affiliate arrangement provides significant volume-based rebate revenue, but requires strict adherence to international and local regulations:

### 4.1 How XM IB Programs Work
* **Model**: XM provides unique tracking links / partner codes. When traders register, deposit, and execute trades on MT4/MT5, XM pays FORTREX an IB commission per lot traded (e.g., $3 to $10 per round-turn lot) or a revenue-share model.
* **Contracting Entity**: The IB partner agreement MUST be signed by **FORTREX Global Entity** (Delaware/UAE/Singapore), NOT the Indian onshore entity (Beyond Pixells Pvt Ltd).

### 4.2 Compliance Constraints & Promises (What FORTREX May/May Not Do)

| Allowed / Safe IB Practices | Strictly Prohibited IB Practices |
| :--- | :--- |
| Prominently disclosing the IB referral relationship ("We receive affiliate commissions from XM"). | Guaranteeing trading profits, pass rates, or funded account payouts based on broker signup. |
| Requiring users to trade via XM demo/simulated accounts for global tournament scoring. | **Soliciting or targeting Indian resident users to open live XM forex accounts.** |
| Providing technical guides on how to connect XM MT4/MT5 read-only API keys to FORTREX leaderboards. | Offering financial advice, trade signals, or account management on behalf of XM traders. |
| Offering entry fee discounts or bonus REX points for non-Indian traders who link verified XM accounts. | Promising that XM trading funds are risk-free or government-insured. |

### 4.3 RBI & FEMA Constraints for Indian Founders
* **RBI Alert List Hazard**: XM, along with over 75 offshore forex platforms (OctaFX, Exness, Olympus Trade), is explicitly named on the **RBI Alert List** of unauthorized forex platforms.
* **FEMA 1999 Prohibition**: Indian residents trading forex on unauthorized offshore platforms violate FEMA guidelines. Any marketing, facilitation, or payment collection originating in India for XM live trading exposes the Indian founder to severe penalties under FEMA and ED investigations.
* **Enforcement Mitigation**:
  1. Maintain strict separation: XM IB referral links must be **geoblocked for all Indian IP addresses**.
  2. Marketing copy targeted at India must focus exclusively on legal domestic trading analytics or pure virtual demo challenges.

### 5.1 Global Payment Stack (Non-India)
* **Recommended Partner**: **Paddle** or **LemonSqueezy** acting as **Merchant of Record (MoR)**.
* **Why MoR is Critical**:
  * **Tax Compliance**: Paddle/LemonSqueezy automatically calculate, collect, and remit EU VAT, US State Sales Taxes, UK VAT, and local digital taxes in over 100 countries.
  * **Card Network Compliance**: As the MoR, Paddle handles buyer chargebacks, PCI-DSS compliance, and fraud monitoring under their merchant account.
  * **Cross-Border FX**: Payouts are remitted in USD via wire transfer to the FORTREX Global bank account (Mercury/Wise/UAE).
* **Onboarding Positioning Strategy**:
  * **Crucial Onboarding Warning**: Paddle and LemonSqueezy strictly reject high-risk merchants, forex brokers, and gambling/wagering platforms.
  * **Application Positioning**: Present FORTREX strictly as a **SaaS Trading Analytics, Tournament Leaderboard & Educational Evaluation Software Platform**. Emphasize that entry fees are for software access/ticket entry into virtual demo challenges, NOT live market deposits or wagering. **`[NEEDS-LAWYER]`**

### 5.2 India Payment Stack (Domestic INR)
* **Recommended Partner**: **Razorpay** or **Cashfree**.
* **Supported Payment Methods**: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Domestic Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking.
* **Compliance Restrictions**:
  * Indian payment gateways strictly prohibit processing transactions related to unauthorized foreign exchange, binary options, or illegal gaming/gambling.
  * Razorpay will flag and suspend accounts attempting to collect entry fees for offshore forex trading challenges.
* **Operational Strategy for India**:
  * Use Razorpay *exclusively* for B2B agency invoicing (Beyond Pixells) or domestic SaaS software analytics tools tied to SEBI-regulated market data (e.g. NSE virtual trading challenges).

## 6. Data Protection, Privacy & Consent Architecture

### 6.1 India DPDP Act 2023 Compliance
Under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India)**, FORTREX acts as a Data Fiduciary regarding user data collected in India:
1. **Notice & Explicit Consent**: Users must give clear, affirmative consent prior to data collection. Consent banners must be available in plain language.
2. **Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary information (e.g., Name, Email, Broker Trading Account ID for verification). Never ask for or store investor account trade passwords or investor investor keys unless necessary for read-only API telemetry.
3. **Data Deletion Rights**: Provide an automated mechanism for users to delete their account and wipe historical trading log data.

### 6.2 Referral & Broker Data Handling
When traders connect MT4/MT5 accounts or register via XM referral links:
* **Read-Only Telemetry**: Tournament tracking must operate strictly via read-only API tokens or MT4/MT5 investor passwords / trade log parsers.
* **Third-Party Data Sharing Disclosure**:
  > *"When you link your broker account, FORTREX accesses read-only execution logs (trades, volume, profit/loss) solely to compute ROI metrics and tournament leaderboard rankings. FORTREX never acquires trade execution capabilities over your broker account."*
