FORTREX is not a registered broker-dealer, financial services provider, investment adviser, portfolio manager, or commodity trading advisor under SEBI, SEC, FCA, or any other financial regulatory authority. All services, leaderboards, and tournament evaluations are intended solely for educational, analytical, and competitive purposes.

1. Non-custodial model

FORTREX never executes trades, holds client funds, or manages capital. Participants trade only on their own accounts at third-party brokers of their choice. FORTREX has no access to participant funds at any time.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and legally permitted to participate in skill-based competitions in your jurisdiction. Tournaments may be restricted or void in certain states and countries. It is your responsibility to verify your local eligibility before entering.

3. Genesis waitlist and REX points

Genesis membership confers early access and launch benefits as described at signup. REX is a non-refundable, non-transferable virtual utility point with no cash value and no mechanism for fiat withdrawal. REX cannot be purchased for money at any stage during the Genesis phase.

4. Fair play

Skill tournaments require honest reporting of trades from a genuine brokerage account. Misrepresentation, fabricated results, or manipulation results in disqualification without refund and permanent removal from the Platform.

5. No advice, no guarantees

Nothing on this Platform constitutes financial, investment, or trading advice, and nothing is a promise of profit. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

For the Genesis waitlist: your name, email address, phone number, country, referral relationships, and signup metadata (timestamp, source page). If you connect a brokerage account for tournament verification at launch: read-only trade history for scoring — never withdrawal or deposit permissions.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We never send marketing that you have not opted into. One launch invite, as promised.
  • We never store broker credentials. Verification uses official, read-only broker APIs where available.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us from the email you registered with. Deletion removes your waitlist entry and referral record.
